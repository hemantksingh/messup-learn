# Directory Overview

This directory contains a collection of Markdown files focused on personal and professional development, with a strong emphasis on engineering, leadership, and related "soft skills." The content appears to be a personal knowledge base or a set of essays on these topics.

## Key Files

*   **`Capabilites.md`**: Outlines the capabilities and competencies for various roles such as Engineering Leadership, Architect, and Software Engineer. It also includes sections on fact-finding and selling a role during recruitment.
*   **`Engineering.md`**: Describes an engineering culture, its values, and principles. Key themes include being value-driven, "you build it you run it," failing fast, and prioritizing non-functionals.
*   **`Influence.md`**: A guide on how to influence others, covering topics like knowing your audience, aligning goals, and building relationships.
*   **`Leadership.md`**: Explores the foundations of leadership, including creating a positive culture, delegation, communication, and motivation.
*   **`Negotiation.md`**: Provides a framework for negotiation, emphasizing focusing on interests over positions and developing a BATNA (Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement).
*   **`Self Awareness.md`**: A deep dive into the concept of self-awareness, how to cultivate it, and its importance in personal and professional life.

## Usage

The content in this directory can be used as a reference for:

*   Understanding and developing a strong engineering culture.
*   Improving leadership and influence skills.
*   Personal growth and self-awareness exercises.
*   Preparing for interviews or discussions on engineering leadership and culture.
*   A source of ideas for blog posts or articles on these topics.
